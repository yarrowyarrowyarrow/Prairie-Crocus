# V3.76 · Doors, downspouts, spigots and mulch rings (F280, the last increment)

The owner: "go ahead with V3.76, mulch rings off by default but available not only in the plan
my yard mode but in planting you do yourself as well. I also want to add spigots to the water
work as many will be using city water." The V3.71 plan's last increment
([its table](V3.71-one-palette.md#the-increments)) was doors, downspouts and mulch rings; the
owner added the spigot, and the rings for hand planting.

## What a person sees

- **Placement › Yard** gains a **Door** (under *Buildings*), and a **Downspout** and a **Spigot**
  (under *Water*). Each is placed with a click and **goes onto the nearest building's wall**
  within 3 m, its long side along the wall, on the outside. Clicked further from any building it
  stands where it was clicked (a yard hydrant is a spigot too). It can be turned and dragged as
  any object since V3.72.
- **A door** is where the 3D's *From the house* stands, looking out from it, and a doorstep the
  planner keeps clear (1.2 m out from the wall). **A downspout** is a pipe down the wall to a
  splash block, which F281 will route water from. **A spigot** is an outdoor tap.
- **Design › Water** says, once a spigot is on the map, how far the planting is from a tap and
  how much of it a garden hose reaches, and how long the first summer's weekly watering takes at
  the tap: the city-water answer, beside the rain-water one it gave before.
- **Mulch rings, off until asked for.** Plan My Yard has *A ring of mulch round each plant*; the
  bar over the map while you place plants or a community has the same box, remembered between
  sessions; and a plant's right-click menu adds or takes away its ring (or its group's). A ring is
  drawn on the map under the plant and in 3D on the ground, and its mulch is in every mulch figure
  (the Overview, the PDF's prep and buy pages, the planting plan).

## The rules (each a stated estimate where the design records nothing; P9)

- **Snapping** (`src/wall_objects.py`, Qt-free): the nearest wall of any building (a drawn
  Building or a scanned footprint, `yard_kinds.is_building_footprint`) within `WALL_SNAP_M`
  (3 m); the object sits `WALL_GAP_M` (5 cm) outside it, its long side along it, and it faces
  away from the building. Sizes: a door 0.9 m wide, a downspout 0.3 m, a spigot 0.2 m.
- **A door's doorstep** is kept out of by the planner: a strip the door's width, 1.2 m out
  (`DOORSTEP_M`). A downspout and a spigot keep nothing out.
- **The house view at a door**: where a door is on the house, the person stands a step out of
  it, facing the planting (the old rule picked the wall most of the planting is nearest to, and
  still does where no door is drawn).
- **Hose reach**: the straight-line distance from each plant to its nearest spigot, against
  `HOSE_M` (15 m and 23 m, the common 50 ft and 75 ft hoses); a hose going round the house needs
  more, which the line says. **Time at the tap**: the first summer's weekly litres (the page's own
  figure) over `TAP_L_MIN` (12 litres a minute, a garden hose on city pressure).
- **A mulch ring** (`src/mulch_rings.py`): about as wide as the plant's spacing, so rings at the
  plant's own spacing just touch, and never under 0.4 m or over 1.5 m across. A plant standing
  in a drawn bed or mulch area gets none: that ground is mulched already. Its mulch is the ring
  area outside the planting area the beds are mulched for, at `site_prep.MULCH_DEPTH_CM`, so no
  ground is counted twice.
- **Which plants have one** is the plant's own `mulch_ring`, carried by `project_store` (the one
  write path): set by the plan's box for its plants, by the placing box for what a person
  places (the store's `mulch_new_plants`, from the box), and by the right-click menu. A swapped
  plant keeps its ring.

## Left out, on purpose

- **Water routed from a downspout** (F281): a downspout is drawn and counted, and nothing yet
  says where its water goes.
- **Water bills.** City water's price differs by city and by tier; the page gives litres and
  minutes, not dollars.
- **A hose's path round the house**: the reach is a straight line, and the words say so.

## What was built, measured

- **On a wall** (`tests/test_doors_taps_and_mulch.py`): a door clicked 1.5 m outside a 10 × 8 m
  house's south wall, or 1 m inside it, stands 0.65 m out from the wall (half its 1.2 m doorstep
  and the 5 cm gap) at the click's place along it, along the wall, facing south; one clicked past
  a corner slides in until all of it is on the wall; one clicked 20 m away stands where it was
  clicked. Its doorstep, kept out of by the planner, runs from the wall to 1.25 m out.
- **In 3D** (`tests/test_yard_render.py`, the real viewer): on the test yard's house a door on
  the back wall stands to 2.2 m, a downspout runs from its splash block on the ground to over
  3 m, and a spigot on the east wall sits between 0.3 and 0.7 m up. *From the house* stands at
  the door, and the check that it stands a step outside the back wall, looking away from it,
  still holds.
- **On the map** (`tests/test_yard_map.py`, headless Chromium): of two rings handed to the map
  the one in a bed is not drawn, the other is, in a pane under the plants; a ringed plant's
  menu offers *Take its mulch ring away* and a bare one *Add a mulch ring*, and choosing it tells
  Python once.
- **Mulch.** A 1.5 m ring under a shrub on the lawn, two overlapping rings counted once, none
  for a wildflower in a bed; the Overview's cost line gains a mulch figure when a plant on a lawn
  is given a ring and none before.
- **The spigot.** With plants 5, 20 and 30 m from a tap: "the farthest plant is 30 m away in a
  straight line. A 15 m (50 ft) hose reaches 1 of 3 plants, a 23 m (75 ft) one 2."

**Where it departs from the plan:** the plan's *Overview offers mulch rings* (the V3.71 plan's Q4
text) became the plant's right-click menu, which reaches plants already placed one at a time or
a group at a time; the placing box sits on the bar over the map rather than in the Plants tab,
so the same box serves a plant and a community.
