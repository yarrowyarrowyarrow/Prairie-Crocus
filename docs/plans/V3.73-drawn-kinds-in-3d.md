# V3.73 · The drawn kinds in 3D (F280, increment 3)

The owner: "go ahead with V3.73". The third of [the V3.71 plan](V3.71-one-palette.md)'s five
increments: **the 3D shows what was drawn, at its outline.** V3.72 drew the kinds the Yard palette
added plainly ([its plan](V3.72-the-yard-palette.md), *The 3D until V3.73*); this increment gives
each its own look, from what the person chose and nothing they did not.

## What V3.72 left

Measured from the scene and the viewer (`tests/test_yard_render.py`, the V3.72 fixture):

- **Plants in a raised bed stand at ground level**, 45 cm down inside its soil, as they stood
  inside the old raised-bed model.
- **A path's or patio's surface is chosen and not shown**: flagstone, pavers, gravel, wood chips
  and stepping stones are all the one `path` look, and pavers, flagstone and concrete the `patio`.
- **Every fence is posts and three rails**, whatever kind was chosen.
- **A pond is a blue sheet with a hard edge** into the lawn; a **rain garden** is a flat dark
  patch; a **swale** a strip of that patch; a **rock garden** a grey texture with no rocks; a
  **deck** the patio's joints.

## What changes

**Python decides, the viewer draws** (the V2.88 lesson), each rule stated in the module that
makes it, and nothing drawn that the design does not record (P9):

- **Plants, flowers, fruit, animals and the walker stand on a raised bed's soil.** The viewer's
  `terrainHeightAt` becomes the ground plus the soil a raised bed lifts at that spot
  (`siteRiseAt`, from the surfaces' `lift_m`, which Python already sends); the ground's own mesh,
  the draped surfaces, the lines and the buildings ask the bare ground (`groundHeightAt`). One
  change at the one function every body already asks, rather than twenty call sites.
- **A look per surface** (the owner's Q6): `flagstone` (irregular stones with joints), `pavers`
  (a running bond), `gravel` (fine specks), `wood_chips` (a mulch, lighter), `stepping_stones`
  (stones on lawn), `concrete` (plain slabs with saw-cut joints). A path or patio with no surface
  chosen (every converted one) keeps its old look. Texture, never geometry (V2.29).
- **A deck is boards**, its joints one way.
- **Fences by kind**: rail (as now), board (posts and a closed panel, 1.8 m), chain link (thin
  posts, a top rail and a see-through mesh, 1.2 m). The heights are stated estimates by kind, as the
  rail fence's 1.2 m always was. A board fence's shade is V3.74 (Q3).
- **A pond has a shore**: a band of pebbles along its outline, half in the water and half on the
  ground (0.5 m, an estimate), so it sits in the ground rather than on it. Ice in winter, as now.
- **A rain garden has a floor**: its outline inset by a fifth of its width (at least 0.3 m) in a
  darker damp look over the planted rim, which reads as a shallow dip without inventing depth.
  A swale gets the same floor down its middle.
- **A rock garden has rocks**: boulders laid by rule inside its outline (P1): about one per 4 m²,
  sized 0.25–0.55 m, never within 0.3 m of its edge, of each other, or of a plant, each placed by
  a seed from the area's id so the same design draws the same rocks.

## Left out, on purpose

- **No depth for a pond, a rain garden or a swale.** The terrain is shared by everything that
  stands on it; carving it would need the ground mesh cut at each outline. The looks say it.
- **A deck's height and a raised bed's plants' roots.** A deck is drawn at ground level: its height
  is not recorded.
- **Hedges as shrubs, and solid fences' shade** (V3.74).

## What was built, measured

`scripts/render_structures.py --sheets kinds --out DIR` draws a new sheet, **kinds**: every kind the
Yard tab stores, three rows of five areas and six lines, July and January, with a close look at
three forbs on a raised bed's soil and at a rock garden's rocks (about 30 seconds). Read from it:
the flagstone, pavers, stepping stones, concrete's joints and the deck's boards each read as what
they are; a pond sits in a pebble shore; a rain garden reads as a dark planted dip; a board fence
is a closed panel and a chain link one a see-through mesh. Two things the first render showed and
this increment then fixed: the rocks were the rock garden's ground colour and could not be seen
(darkened), and the sheet's rows stood in front of each other (spaced so each tile shows its own).

`tests/test_yard_render.py` reads it back from the real viewer: a forb in a raised bed stands at
the soil (base above 0.44 m) and the same forb beside it at the ground; a flagstone path is not the
plain path's colour; the viewer draws every rock the scene laid, the pond's shore and two floors;
a board fence is closed and its posts 1.85 m, a chain link one see-through at 1.25 m.

**Where it departs from the plan:** a pond's shore is drawn as one strip a side, which is how the
viewer already draws a swale or a road. In January every hard surface is packed snow, as V3.45
decided for paths and paving, so the surfaces look alike under it; the rocks take snow on top.
The 3D timeline calls a raised bed a "raised bed" (it called every bed a "garden bed", so a raised
bed in a garden bed read as a second one).

## What could not be checked here

- The looks are read back from the real viewer in headless Chromium (their names, colours, the
  rocks' count and spacing, a plant's base on a raised bed); whether they read well at a person's
  eye height is for the contact sheet and the owner.
