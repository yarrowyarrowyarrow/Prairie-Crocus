# V3.61 · The photographs come back, and two branches' lost commits with them

*The owner, 19 November 2026: "at some point the plant pane regressed and we lost the 6 pics that
were showing up. and not all plants have pics even so that is a regression".*

## What happened

The photographs had not been deleted; **they were never on the branches after V3.55**.

- V3.55 was two sessions' work on one branch. One session pushed the Design tab review, ending at
  `48b63dc9` (02:52, 18 Nov). Another pushed two more commits after it: *the Edmonton natives'
  photographs arrive* (`0fbd23fa`, 03:09: 1,482 photographs, six for 241 of the 250 natives,
  schema v102) and *the tests the photographs moved* (`87f716fc`, 03:42).
- V3.56 was cut from this session's copy of V3.55, which stopped at `48b63dc9`. V3.56 to V3.60
  were each cut from the one before, so none had the photographs. `data/plant_photos.json` went
  back to its two rows, the plant page showed one photo from `plants_master.json`'s `image_url`,
  and the 62 natives that only the fold had given a photo showed none.
- **Measured over every release branch from V3.50**: each contains the whole of the one before,
  except two. **V3.55 lacks V3.54's last two commits** in the same way: *Fill the gaps fills with
  the wider low plants* (`e86a2afc`, a real fix in `yard_overview._fillers`) and a plan note
  (`6ac8b77d`).
- **Both lost pairs fixed tests.** Four of the nine failures this session called pre-existing
  since V3.56 (`test_month_tasks` ×2, `test_what_it_feeds` ×2) were fixed in those commits. They
  were not pre-existing. They were the visible sign of the missing commits, and nobody followed
  them up.
- V3.55's photographs and V3.59's communities both claimed **schema v102**. An install that
  reached v102 by one of them would never reseed for the other, because `init_db` reseeds only
  when the shipped number is higher than the database's.

## What was done

1. The four commits cherry-picked onto V3.61 in their original order. Three conflicts:
   - the schema comment: both histories kept;
   - the schema-change skill's version line: V3.59's text plus this one;
   - three Overview tests, which V3.55 and V3.54 had each rewritten for the new example: V3.55's
     newer form kept.
2. **Schema v103**, past both v102s, so every install reseeds once and gets the photographs and the
   Edmonton communities, whichever v102 it holds.
3. **A guard**, `tests/test_plant_photos.TestTheShippedPhotographs`: every Edmonton native has a
   photograph in the shipped file, and at least 90% have six. Run against V3.60's file it fails,
   listing the natives without one. The suite had stayed green through five releases without the
   photographs, because nothing said how many there should be.
4. CLAUDE.md's branch section: before cutting the next branch, fetch the previous one by refspec
   and check it is an ancestor of `HEAD`.

On a fresh database: the 250 Edmonton natives have 6 photographs (241), 5 (3), 4 (4), 3 (1) and
2 (1). **None has no photograph.** Water Birch's page has six.

## Left alone

- **The 113 catalogue plants with no photograph** are all off the Edmonton list. The catalogue has
  been the Edmonton list in both apps since V3.58, so no plant a person can open is among them.
- The V3.35 picking page can still replace a plant's six with the owner's own choice
  (`fold_photo_picks.py`), plant by plant.

## Could not verify

The photographs themselves, on a real desktop: this container cannot draw a remote image into the
page. The rows and the page's list of six are checked; the pixels are not.
