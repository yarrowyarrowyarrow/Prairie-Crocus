# V3.71 · One way to put things on the map (F280): the plan

**The owner:** "yes to both, plan F280." *Both* are the last two questions of
[the V3.69 structures review](V3.69-structures-and-shapes-reviewed.md): **a shed becomes a
building** (drawn, casting shade, a gable roof), and **a hedge becomes a row of real native
shrubs**. The first two were answered before V3.70: **one palette of three verbs** (draw an area,
draw a line, place an object) with Structures as its groups, and **old designs keep what the map
showed**.

This is the plan for all five increments, and **V3.71 also carries the first** (*One table*):
the owner, having read it, answered the first three questions below ("yes to both" to the palette
and to fences and hedges in the simple app with solid ones casting shade) and said "go ahead with
V3.71". What it built, and where it departed from this plan, is [at the end](#v371-one-table-built).

  this plan gives it the kinds to work with.)

## The idea

**Every kind of thing in a yard is one row of one table**, and every part of the app asks that
table, so a pond is a pond to the score, the Overview, the planner, the Water page, the pond
planting, the shade map and the 3D, however it was put on the map. A person meets the table as
**one palette** with three verbs: *draw an area*, *draw a line*, *place an object*. Anything with
an outline is drawn at its outline; anything that is an object is placed, then moved and turned.

**What a person sees.** One palette, in groups (*Beds and ground*, *Water*, *For wildlife*,
*Fences and hedges*, *Buildings*, *Around the yard*), each kind with its verb on its button
(*Draw on the map* or *Place on the map*), its few options (a surface, a raised height, a shrub)
and nothing else: no fill colour, opacity or line style except on Custom. *Casts shade, height*
leaves every area but Building, which ends the Garden Bed trap.

**Where it lives (Q1).** Placement gets a third tab beside Plants and Communities, replacing
Structures; Draw › Shape retires, and the Draw row keeps Boundary, Measure, Note and Select.
Placement is where a person puts things in the yard, the panel has room for a kind's words and
options, and the Shape form was a 300 px menu that the colour dialog closed under it.

## One table every part of the app asks

A Qt-free module, `src/yard_kinds.py`, holds the table: each kind's key, words, group, verb,
the names it had (shape types, structure ids, line styles), its options, and the answers the
readers need. **The readers stop holding their own lists**:

| Reader | Asks today | Asks then |
|---|---|---|
| `yard_overview` (planting area) | `BED_SHAPES`, `NOT_PLANTED` by shape words | each kind's planting answer |
| `exclusion` (keep-outs, fill zones) | `EXCLUDING_STRUCTURE_IDS`, the Overview's lists | the same answer |
| `zoning`, `llm_design` | `WET_STRUCTURE_IDS` | a kind's ground (wet for a rain garden, dry for a rock garden) |
| `habitat_score` | `HABITAT_STRUCTURE_IDS` by structure id | a kind's habitat type |
| `design_inputs`, `water_budget` | `WATER_STRUCTURES` by structure id | a kind's water role |
| `pond_planting`, `pond_habit` | the Pond structure's ellipse | any pond's outline |
| `shade` | buildings by element type | a kind's shade answer (buildings, board fences, walls) |
| `cues_to_care` | `_LAWN_SHAPES`, `_LAWN_STRUCTURES`, `_BORDER_STRUCTURES` | a kind's cue |
| `scene_site`, `scene_structures`, the viewer | `SURFACE_KINDS`, struct ids | a kind's look |
| `planting_plan`, `sourcing`, `maintenance_calendar` | a fixed cost and hours per structure | the same ranges, scaled by size from the catalogue's default (stated estimates, P9) |

A guard test, like `tests/test_region_guard.py`, fails on a structure id or a shape type written
as a literal outside the table, so a new reader cannot grow its own list again.

**Nothing in the score moves on conversion except where this plan says so**: a drawn Water
Feature gains the pond's +2, because it is a pond. F256 already plans structure points by habitat
value (its D3); this plan keeps today's points and adds the drawn kinds to that question.

## Old designs

The file format goes to **1.11** (`src/project.py`, `SCHEMA_VERSION`): areas and lines carry a
`kind`, objects a `bearing_deg`. A Qt-free `src/yard_upgrade.py` converts a design when it is
opened, by every door a design comes in (the window, recovery, the saves list, the scripting API,
the example), **at the outline the map showed** (the owner's answer):

- Pond, Rain Garden, Rock Xeriscape (a circle on the map) → the area of that circle.
- Raised Bed (45 cm), Native Lawn Patch (a north-south rectangle on the map) → that rectangle.
- Bioswale → a swale line along the rectangle's length, at its width.
- Fence / Wall → a rail fence along the rectangle's length (as V3.70 draws it).
- Tool Shed → a building on its rectangle, 2.4 m.
- Water Feature → a pond on the same outline.
- A Garden Bed or Mulch Area with a height up to 1.2 m → a raised bed at that height; any other
  drawn shape with a height (not a tree canopy) → a building at it, which is what it already was
  to the shade map.
- Patio / Deck → a patio; Pathway → a path with no surface chosen, drawn as today.
- A hedge with a species written in → kept as it was. **No plant is placed without the person**
  (Q5): its species is matched against the region's list, and the Overview offers *Plant this
  hedge* with the match.
- Objects stay objects, facing as the map drew them.

Conversion is idempotent and **does not mark the design changed**: opening and closing without
saving asks nothing, and the file stays 1.10 until the person saves. The shapes in each design
keep a legacy shape type where one is close (a pond as Water Feature, a raised bed as Garden Bed),
so an older copy of the app opening a saved 1.11 file still draws the outline.

**What changes in an old design's numbers, on purpose:** a converted pond or rain garden comes off
the Overview's planting area; a raised bed, rock garden or rain garden becomes ground Plan My Yard
plants (into, not around); a native lawn patch becomes ground it keeps out of (it planted over
it); a shed becomes a building that casts shade and is kept out of. Each is a test on a fixture
design holding every structure.

## Hedges as real plants

Drawing a hedge asks for **a shrub from the region's list** (a living fence two or three, a
windbreak trees and tall shrubs in two rows), and its spacing is the shrub's own. When the line is
finished its plants are placed along it through `project_store`, the way the Row pattern places a
row, as **one undo step**, grouped under the line: delete the line and they go with it, move it
(F279) and they move. They are then plants like any other: the buy list counts them, the score and
the food pages read them, the 3D draws them as the shrubs V3.49 built, the shade map and the wind
shadow (`wind_shadow.py`, which works from real plants) see them. The line itself draws nothing in
3D. The free-text species box goes.

## Objects

An object is placed, then **moved** (dragged, as an existing tree is today) and **turned** (R, or
*Turn 45°* on its right-click menu, as a community turns, `11b-turn.js`), and its Size box says
what it measures (V3.70). A bee hotel's face matters to the bees, so turning it is the point. A
**door** and a **downspout** snap to the nearest wall of a building: a door is where *From the
house* stands (`scene_views.house_view` picks a wall today) and where a path can start; a
downspout is where F281's water comes from. The objects' models are F288's part 2, after this.

## Mulch round the plants (Q4)

An option in Plan My Yard and on the Overview's *what it will take* item, off by default: a ring
of mulch under each plant outside a mulched bed, on the map and in 3D, costed at
`site_prep.MULCH_DEPTH_CM` like the beds.

## V3.71: One table, built

`src/yard_kinds.py` is the table: 28 kinds, each with the names it has had (shape types, structure
ids, line styles) and its answers, and four questions every reader now asks of a feature:
`kind_of`, `is_fill`, `is_keep_out` and `footprint_ring` (a placed structure's outline as
`placeStructureOnMap` draws it). `structure_defs` is what the score, the Water page, the care hours,
the buy list, the PDF and the lessons are handed: every placed structure's definition, and a drawn
Water Feature as the catalogue's pond, marked `drawn`.

**The readers that now ask it:** the Overview (`yard_overview`: `BED_SHAPES`, `NOT_PLANTED` and a
placed structure's footprint as a bed or a hole), the planner's keep-outs and fill zones
(`exclusion`), wet ground (`zoning`), the habitat score's types (`habitat_score`), the Water page
(`design_inputs`), the 3D looks (`scene_site.SURFACE_KINDS`), the care cues (`cues_to_care`), the
"is it a bed?" checks in Plan My Yard's *Anywhere in my yard* (`controllers/generation`), the
generator's beds box (`llm_design`) and the first-run check (`onboarding`), the pond planting
(`pond_planting.ponds_in`, a `DrawnPond` for a drawn one), and the eight places that built their
own list of structures (`app.py` three times, `permadesign_api.Project.structures`, the Overview's
cost item, the PDF twice, the lessons).

**Every derived list that was not meant to change is pinned to the value it had**
(`tests/test_yard_kinds.py`), and they are byte-for-byte the old ones.

**What changed, measured** (`scripts/render_structures.py --readers`, and Plan My Yard on a 12 x 12 m
yard with first-run goals at 70% cover):

| On the map | Before (V3.70) | Now |
|---|---|---|
| A placed pond | counted as ground to plant (144 m²) | off the planting area (116 m²), like a drawn one |
| A drawn Water Feature | no score, not a pond to the Water page or the pond planting | +2 as a pond, a pond to the Water page, seats the plan's water plants |
| A placed raised bed, Size 6 m | ignored: 233 plants, 5 inside it | a bed: **19 plants, all inside it** (with *In the beds I drew*; *Anywhere in my yard* still offered) |
| A tool shed | 233 plants, 3 inside it | **0 inside**; off the planting area |
| A fire pit | 233 plants, 2 inside it | 0 inside |
| A native lawn patch | 233 plants, 28 on it | 0 on it; off the planting area, as a drawn lawn |
| A rock xeriscape | ignored | a bed (dry ground, for F281) |
| A placed raised bed and the care cues | not an edge | a bed's edge, as a drawn bed is |

**Where it departs from the plan, and why:**

- **A rain garden stays kept out of**, not planted. The planner's cells carry no moisture signal
  (`placement_score.CellEnv` is shade, elevation, slope and aspect), so "plants that take wet feet"
  cannot be chosen yet, and ordinary plants on a rain garden's floor would be worse than none.
  The table records it as wet ground; F281 plants it.
- **The objects are kept out of**, where the plan's table said "as now": a plan was putting plants
  in a fire pit and a compost bin. A bee hotel's footprint is small, but on a 12 m yard with one
  in the middle the plan has 222 plants instead of 233, because the drifts round it move.
- **A shed does not cast shade yet.** A shade caster here would need a second path into the Sun &
  Shade list of what casts (`sun_shade.caster_rows`), which V3.72 removes by making the shed a
  building, already a caster everywhere. So V3.72 does it once.
- **Costs and hours are not scaled by size yet.** The buy list's two cost paths read the catalogue
  and the stored definition in opposite orders (`planting_plan._structures_detail` and
  `sourcing.structure_cost`), so scaling one would make them disagree. A drawn pond costs and takes
  the hours a placed one does; scaling comes with V3.72, where sizes are drawn.

**Found on the way: the review's Plan My Yard numbers were measured without `shapely`**, which the
planner's fill-to-cover step needs and every install has. Re-measured with it, the raised bed and the
shed findings stand; the Garden Bed with a height does not plant round the bed (0 of 45) but plants
the bed and the lawn round it (72 of 117 inside). The review and this plan are corrected.

**Tests:** `tests/test_yard_kinds.py` (18): the table covers every structure, shape type, zone and
line style once; the pinned lists; each pair agreeing through the real readers; footprints as the
map draws them; a drawn pond's seats; Plan My Yard on a placed raised bed (every plant inside) and
a shed (none inside); and a ratchet over the literal structure ids and shape words left in `src/`
outside the table's owners, which may only shrink. `tests/test_exclusion.py`'s bee-hotel case
states the new rule.
