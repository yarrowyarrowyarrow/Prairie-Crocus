# V3.55 · The natives' photographs arrive

**The owner:** "Look into why some plants (Edmonton native) do not have photos. I believe I
selected the photos that were most appropriate for each of these species … There should be good
pics for each of these 250." Then, on the options below: "Lets do option B."

## What was found

- **No pick had ever been folded in.** `data/plant_photos.json` held 2 rows for the whole
  catalogue. Of the 250 reviewed Edmonton natives, **62 showed no photo** and the other 188 the one
  photo the old fetch (`fetch_inaturalist_images.py`) had found. None showed more than one, though
  the V3.35 strip and gallery have been ready since V3.35.
- **The candidates were there.** The owner ran `fetch_photo_candidates.py` and committed
  `data/fetched/photo_candidates.json` (V3.47): all 250 natives, at least 4 photographs each, every
  one CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA.
- **The picking page's file never reached a session.** The V3.35 path ends with *Save my picks*
  writing `photo_picks.json` on the owner's PC and a session folding it; none was ever sent, and
  none is in the repository.
- **The selection the owner remembered was not found.** The owner described a simpler step
  before V3.35: one best photo a plant from the viable options. No plan, data file or published
  page in this repository records one (searched: every plan, `data/`, `data/fetched/`, the colour
  checks, the bench, the owner's artifacts). It may have happened outside the repository.
- **What did exist was V3.48's reading of every photograph**, made for the 3D models:
  `data/fetched/model_review.json` keeps, per native, the good references by what each shows
  (habit, leaf, flower, fruit, winter, bark): 2,771 of 3,288, all among the candidates. Nothing
  showed them as photographs.

## Options put to the owner

| | Option | |
|---|---|---|
| A | Fold the owner's own `photo_picks.json`, if one exists on the PC | not found |
| **B** | **V3.48's references as they are**, up to six a plant, the flower first | **chosen** |
| C | The picking page's own suggestion for every plant (`photo_picks.prepick`) | |

The caution given with B: the references were chosen to show *form* for the 3D work, so some
lead photographs are plain (a seed head held in a hand, a leaf close-up), where the V3.35 rule is
that the owner confirms each plant. A contact sheet of all 250 leads went to the owner to flag
any to swap.

## What was done

- **`scripts/picks_from_model_review.py`** writes the references in the picking page's format:
  one of each kind in turn (flower, whole plant, fruit, winter, leaves, bark) in the order the
  review kept them, at most six (`photo_picks.MAX_PICKS`), so the flower leads (V3.35's D2).
  Two rules added on looking at the leads:
  - **A tree, shrub or vine with no showy flower leads with the whole plant**, from the colour
    review's own verdict (`no_showy_flower`): Paper Birch led with a twig in somebody's hand, Green
    Alder with a catkin. Eight plants; the four conifers already led that way.
  - **A CC0 credit names the uploader.** The candidates carry "no rights reserved" alone; the
    login is appended ("…, uploaded by troymc"), the form the older photographs already had.
- **`fold_photo_picks.py --write`**, unchanged: all 250 pass its checks; **1,482 photographs**
  folded, 241 natives with six, Vasey's Rush with the two iNaturalist holds under an open licence.
  232 lead with a flower, 17 with the whole plant, 1 with fruit.
- **Schema v102**, so installed copies reseed and show them. The 174 plants not on the Edmonton
  list keep their one photo; 51 of them still have none.
- `tests/test_photo_picks.py`: every native has a photograph, and the shipped files are exactly
  what the script makes, so a hand edit of a native's photos fails until the script agrees.

## Branch

V3.55 had already been published as a prerelease when this was folded in. Pushing it to V3.55
replaces that prerelease's installers; a copy that installed the earlier V3.55 will not be offered
this one by the updater, which compares versions only. Nothing has been promoted since, so only
the owner's own test copy is affected: reinstall it from the release page, or move this to V3.56.

## Not verified

- **The photographs on a real screen.** The seeded database returns six for a native, flower
  first (checked); the strip itself was tested in V3.35 on local images, not on these.
- **Each photograph's choice for display.** The 250 leads were looked at on a contact sheet;
  the other 1,232 were judged as 3D references, not as display photographs.
