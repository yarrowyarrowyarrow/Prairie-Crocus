# V3.75 · The Yard tab tidied, and a hedge that is already there (F280)

The owner, after trying V3.72 to V3.74: lines "don't close when you click to do so the way a bed
or path does, for example a swale"; areas should place as a circle or a rectangle with its
dimensions typed, as well as by drawing the outline; only every second row of the Yard list
highlights; the list is messy and wants collapsing groups; and a hedge you plant duplicates
planting a row of plants: "most useful for showing a hedge that already exists, which in Edmonton
is most likely cotoneaster or sometimes caragana". This increment does those five. The V3.71
plan's last increment (doors, downspouts and mulch rings) moves to **V3.76**.

## The owner's answers (asked before the work)

1. **Living fence and Windbreak leave the Yard tab** (recommended): the same duplication as the
   hedge. A row of plants is planted with the Plants tab's Row.
2. **An existing hedge is one of**: Cotoneaster, Caragana, Common lilac, Cedar, or Other.
3. **A rectangle with typed dimensions follows the cursor and R turns it 15° before the click**
   (recommended), as a community turns while it is placed.

## What changes

- **A line finishes the way an area does.** Clicking its last point finishes it; clicking its
  first point closes it into a loop (a fence or a hedge round the yard) and finishes it, within
  the 12 px an area's first point takes. A double-click and Shift+Enter still finish. The words
  under the map say so. A double-click had also left the line's last point doubled; it no longer
  does.
- **An area is placed as its outline, a rectangle or a circle.** Every area in the Yard tab has
  *Place it as*: Outline (drawn as before), Rectangle (length and width in metres) or Circle (its
  width across). A rectangle or circle follows the cursor, R turns a rectangle 15°, a click puts
  it down, and it is stored as the same outline a drawn one is (so everything that reads a drawn
  area reads it). Sizes start at a stated guess per kind (`yard_kinds.SHAPE_SIZE_M`, a 3 × 1.2 m
  bed, a 5 × 1 m path, a 3 m pond); every one is the person's to change.
- **The list highlights every row.** Its alternate-row colour had been declared after the
  selected one, so on every second row it won.
- **The list collapses.** Seven groups, closed when the tab opens and each showing how many kinds
  it holds: *Beds and ground* (bed, mulch, lawn, path, patio, deck), *Lawn conversion* (the five
  stages, which had been half of the first group), *Water*, *For wildlife*, *Fences and hedges*
  (fence, wall, hedge), *Buildings*, *Around the yard*. Choosing a kind from the Overview opens
  its group.
- **A hedge is a hedge already growing.** Drawn along its middle, with what it is (the owner's
  five), its height and its width, each starting at a stated guess for the kind
  (`yard_kinds.HEDGE_SIZE_M`: a clipped cotoneaster 1.5 × 1.0 m, caragana 2.5 × 1.5 m, lilac
  2.5 × 2.0 m, cedar 3.0 × 1.0 m, other 1.5 × 1.0 m). It is not a plant: it is not counted in the
  score, the food pages or the buy list. It **casts shade** at its height (with a deciduous
  hedge's leaf-off break, as a deciduous tree's crown has, and the cedar's all year), **is kept
  out of** by Plan My Yard, is drawn in 3D as the hedge mass at its height and width in its own
  green (bare in winter, the cedar not), and is a plain line on the map in that green, no dots.
- **V3.74's planting goes.** `src/hedge_planting.py`, `src/hedge_flow.py`, the plant lists in
  the Yard tab, *Plant this hedge…* on the map and the Overview's offer are removed. The wall's
  and the board fence's shade (Q3) stays.

## Old designs (`src/yard_upgrade.py`)

- A hedge, living fence or windbreak line (any copy before V3.75) becomes an existing hedge, at
  the height the 3D drew it (a hedge 1.2 m, a living fence 1.6 m, a windbreak 3.0 m) and the width
  it was drawn, what it is read from the species typed on it (cotoneaster, caragana, lilac, cedar
  or arborvitae or thuja), else Other. The typed words stay on it.
- A line V3.74 planted (its `plant_ids`, with plants grouped under it) is taken off: its plants
  stay, as plants. One with `plant_ids` and no plants left is an existing hedge like any other.
- Idempotent and never an undo step, as every conversion on open.

## Left out, on purpose

- **A hedge's plants as plants.** Cotoneaster, caragana, lilac and cedar are not in the
  catalogue (it is the region's natives and a garden list); an existing hedge is a feature, as an
  existing tree is.
- **A hedge's leaf-off shade in the shade map's grid is the tree crowns' weight**; nothing
  measures a hedge's own.
- **Doors, downspouts and mulch rings**: V3.76.

## What was built, measured

- **The highlight.** On V3.74's palette the chosen row's colour, read off the list in four rows
  running, was `#1e2f1e`, `#2f5a2f`, `#1e2f1e`, `#2f5a2f`: the alternate rows' background won. On
  V3.75 all four read the same green (`tests/test_yard_palette.py` reads them in pixels).
- **On the real map** (`tests/test_yard_map.py`, headless Chromium): a swale drawn with three
  clicks finishes on a click of its last point with three points (no doubled end); a fence closed
  on its first point has four, the last the first; a double-click's quick second click adds and
  finishes nothing, and the double-click finishes the line; six kinds of area each close on their
  first corner; a 4 × 2 m rectangle turned once by R lands as four corners 4.0 and 2.0 m apart,
  its long side 15° east of north, 8.0 m²; a 3 m circle is 32 points 1.50 m from its centre.
- **The hedge's shade.** A 2.5 m caragana hedge 1.5 m wide shades about 3 m of ground north of it;
  on 21 December a cedar the same size shades the ground just north of it more than the bare
  caragana does. A full-sun penstemon a metre north of a cotoneaster hedge is said to be shaded
  by "the cotoneaster hedge" in the timeline.
- **Old designs.** A hedge, living fence or windbreak line becomes an existing hedge at 1.2,
  1.6 or 3.0 m, what it is read from its words ("Peking cotoneaster", "Siberian peashrub",
  "Syringa", "thuja"); a V3.74 line with its plants on it comes off and the plants stay.

- **Plan My Yard's review lot** (`scripts/render_yard.py`) carries a hedge along its east side,
  which since V3.45 was left out of the planning so the contact sheets stayed comparable. Once the
  hedge cast shade, the probe found four plants in more shade than they take: the app hands every
  drawn line to the planner, so the lot is now planned with it, and the probe's rules hold again
  (0 in more shade than they take).
- A hedge's shade caster is its own kind, `hedge`, extruded as a building is everywhere (every
  reader asks only whether a caster is a tree): `tests/test_leaf_off_shade.py` holds that a
  building never takes the leaf-off break, whatever it carries.

**Where it departs from the plan:** *Place it as* is remembered from one area to the next (a
person placing three raised beds should not choose Rectangle three times), and the map takes the
keyboard when a rectangle or circle is being placed, because R reaches only a focused map and the
button the person pressed was in the side panel.

## What could not be checked here

- **The real window.** This container has no QtWebEngine: the palette was driven offscreen and the
  map page in headless Chromium, but R pressed in the app, with the map focused by `mode.py`, was
  not.
- **Row in the simple app.** The owner's reason for retiring the planted hedge was that a row can
  be planted with the Plants tab's Row; Row, Grid and Circle are behind the Advanced tools
  (V3.21). Whether Row belongs in the simple app is a question for the owner.
