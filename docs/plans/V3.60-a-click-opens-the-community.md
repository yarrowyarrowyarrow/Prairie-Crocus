# V3.60 · A click opens the community (F275)

*The owner, 19 November 2026, with a screenshot of Placement › Communities: "This info to the right
of the community names is not very useful/necessary for selecting them (I guess sunlight could be
useful for a filter) and therefore should be moved/removed. I'd also like the images to return,
not sure where the community individual plant images went but seeing the photos of the plants in
the community then being able to click an individual plant to open it's description would be
good."*

## What was found

- **The photographs had not gone.** The community's page (V3.09: its plants as photograph tiles,
  each opening the plant's page) still opened, in this build, on a click with nothing being placed.
  Asked, the owner answered that *no page opens* when they click a community, and that the photos
  belong on the page, not in the list.
- **Why no page opened.** After *Place*, the map stays ready to place more until Esc. In that state
  a click in the list did not open a page: it changed which community the next map click would
  place (the V2.99 rule, "the list is a palette while placing"), and nothing said so. Reproduced in
  the real window: Place, then click another community: `mode polyculture`, no page. Having just
  placed and turned communities (V3.59), that is what the owner met every time.
- **The facts column** ("14 plants of 7 kinds · Full sun · Dry") took a third of each row and
  folded most names onto two lines. Sun and soil are already filters; the counts and conditions are
  on the community's page.

## What changed

- **A click is looking, in both states** (`polyculture_panel._on_list_choose` always asks for the
  page; `community_flyout._on_community_requested` ends any placing first, `main._cancel_draw`).
  Enter or a double-click places, as before. The plant list keeps the V2.99 palette rule: the owner
  raised only the communities.
- **The rows are names.** The second column is empty, so `two_line_rows.fit` gives it a few
  pixels and the names the row. The facts lead each row's tooltip and stay at the top of the page.

## Tests

`test_app_smoke.test_a_click_after_placing_opens_the_page_and_stops_placing` does what the owner
did: Place, a real mouse click on another community, then the page is showing that one and the map
is no longer placing. Three tests that held the old behaviour were rewritten to the new rule
(`test_community_page`, `test_placement_arming`, `test_communities_compare`).

## Could not verify

The photographs themselves: this container is offline, so the tiles show each kind's colour. The
tile's photo path was checked with a stand-in download (each tile asks for its photo and is
redrawn when it lands).
