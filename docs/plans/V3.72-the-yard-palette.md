# V3.72 · The Yard palette (F280, increment 2)

The owner: "those surfaces are fine, go ahead with V3.72", after answering Q1 (a Placement tab
called **Yard**, Draw › Shape retired), Q2 (fences and hedges in the simple app), Q3 (solid fences
and walls cast shade, built in V3.74) and Q6 (the surfaces). The design is
[the V3.71 plan](V3.71-one-palette.md); V3.71 built its table (`src/yard_kinds.py`). This
increment is what a person sees: **one place to put things in a yard, three verbs, and old designs
converted to it when they open.**

## What changes for a person

- **Placement › Yard** replaces Placement › Structures, and **Draw › Shape is gone**. The Draw row
  keeps Boundary, Measure, Note and Select.
- The Yard tab is one list under six headings (*Beds and ground*, *Water*, *For wildlife*,
  *Fences and hedges*, *Buildings*, *Around the yard*). Choosing a kind shows a sentence about
  it, its few options, and one button with its verb: **Draw on the map** for an area or a line,
  **Place on the map** for an object.
- **Options, and only these** (Q6): a path's surface (flagstone, pavers, gravel, wood chips,
  stepping stones); a patio's (pavers, flagstone, concrete); a deck is wood; a bed at ground or
  raised 30, 45, 60 or 90 cm; a fence rail, board or chain link; a building a house, garage or
  shed, with its height (the **only** height box left, which ends the Garden Bed trap of V3.69); a
  swale's width; a hedge's width, spacing and what is planted (until V3.74 makes it real shrubs);
  an object's size (V3.70's ranges). Fill colour, outline, opacity and line style only on
  *Custom*, which stays an advanced tool.
- **Fences and hedges are in the simple app** (Q2). Until now a line was advanced-only.
- **An object can be moved and turned**: dragged when no tool is on, as an existing tree is; turned
  45° by **R** with the pointer over it or *Turn 45°* on its right-click menu, which now also holds
  *Remove* (a right-click used to remove at once). Each is one undo step. A rectangle is drawn
  turned on the map, and the 3D turns its model to match (V3.70's bearing, now read from the
  feature).
- **The shed is a building**: drawn at its outline, casting shade on the shade map, listed in
  Sun & Shade's casters, drawn with the scan's buildings in 3D, and kept out of by Plan My Yard
  (it was, since V3.71; it now also casts).

## How things are stored (file format 1.11)

| Verb | Feature | Fields added |
|---|---|---|
| Area | `custom_shape` Polygon, as Draw › Shape wrote | `kind`; the kind's options: `raised_cm` (a bed), `surface` (a path or patio) |
| Building | `canopy_footprint` Polygon with `cast_shade`, `height_m`, `canopy_radius_m` and `shape_type` "Building", **as a scanned building is**, so the shade map, the house view, the Overview's front and back and the 3D's roofs read it unchanged | `kind: building`, `building` (house, garage, shed) |
| Line | `hedgerow` LineString | `kind`; `fence_kind` (rail, board, chain link); a swale's and a hedge's `width_m` |
| Object | `structure` Point, as before | `bearing_deg` (absent means as the map always drew it, north) |

`yard_kinds.kind_of` reads `kind` first, then the old names (a shape type, a structure id, a line
style), so a feature from any version is read the same way. **Every drawn area keeps a shape type
an older copy of the app knows where one is close** (a raised bed as Garden Bed, a deck as Patio /
Deck, a native lawn as Lawn Area, a rock garden as Garden Bed, a pond as Water Feature; a rain
garden as Custom, since nothing older is close), so a 1.11 file opened by V3.71 still draws every
outline and plants round the right things.

## Old designs, converted on open

`src/yard_upgrade.py` (Qt-free) converts a design **at the outline the map showed** (the owner's
answer), by every door a design comes in: the window's open, recovery, the saves list, the
scripting API's `load`, and a plan that lands (the generator still places a pond or a brush pile
as a structure, because its scoring and its pond seating work on them; they are converted as they
reach the map).

| On the map before | After |
|---|---|
| Pond, Rain Garden, Rock Xeriscape (a circle of `size_m`) | that circle, as a pond, rain garden or rock garden area |
| Raised Bed (a rectangle) | that rectangle, as a bed raised 45 cm |
| Native Lawn Patch (a rectangle) | that rectangle, as a native lawn |
| Tool Shed (a rectangle) | a building on that rectangle, a shed 2.4 m tall |
| Bioswale (a rectangle) | a swale line along its length, at its width |
| Fence / Wall (a rectangle) | a rail fence along its length (as V3.70 draws it) |
| Water Feature | a pond, on the same outline |
| A Garden Bed or Mulch Area with a height up to 1.2 m | a bed raised to that height, no longer casting shade |
| Any other drawn shape with a height (not a tree canopy) | a building at that height, which it already was to the shade map |
| Every other area and line | the same feature with its `kind` |
| Objects | unchanged; they face north until turned |

Conversion is idempotent, **is not an undo step and does not mark the design changed**: opening and
closing without saving asks nothing, and the file stays 1.10 until it is saved.

## Found on the way: a saved line came back twice

**Opening a design with a hedge, fence, living fence or windbreak added a second copy of it.**
The map draws a saved line through the same function that finishes a drawn one
(`loadHedgerow` → `finishHedgerow`), and that function told Python a line had been drawn. Python
added it to the design, put it on the undo stack and marked the design changed. Measured in
headless Chromium with the real map: loading one saved fence sends one `onHedgerowComplete`. So
each open-and-save doubled the lines (the example yard's two fences became four, then eight); a
design opened and closed untouched asked to be saved; and the line the map knew was the copy, so
right-clicking a reopened fence removed the copy and left the original in the file. Shapes have
had a guard for this since V1.58 (`_suppressBridge`); lines never got one. Fixed the same way,
and the line keeps its stored id.

## The 3D until V3.73

V3.73 draws every kind at its outline. Until then nothing that drew may stop drawing, and the
converted kinds take the plainest honest look:

- a pond is water at its outline (a drawn Water Feature's look since V3.45; the GLB's stone rim
  goes, and floating plants lie on the water at ground level, as they do in a drawn pond now);
- a raised bed is its soil lifted to its height inside a plain frame along its outline;
- a rock garden, a rain garden, a native lawn and a deck each get a surface look; a swale is a
  strip of the rain garden's look along its line, at its width;
- a wall is a plain grey slab along its line; every fence kind is still posts and rails;
- a building is drawn as the scan's buildings are, with `roof_for`'s gable on a clean house-sized
  rectangle.

## Costs and hours by size

`yard_kinds.structure_defs` stamps a `scale` on what it hands the buy list and the care hours: a
drawn kind's area, or a line's length, over its catalogue default's (a 6 m pond, 10 m of swale),
**to a tenth** (the ranges are estimates, and a converted 6 m pond, its circle drawn with 32 sides,
came out at 0.99 to two places and cost 1% less). `planting_plan._structures_detail`,
`sourcing.structure_cost` and the care hours (`maintenance_calendar._structure_hours`) all multiply
by it, so the two cost paths agree (V3.71 left scaling out because they read the definition in
opposite orders). Measured: every converted kind keeps exactly its V3.71 cost and hours (pond
$400–2,000 and 8 h; rain garden, rock garden, native lawn, swale, raised bed likewise); a drawn
7 × 8 m pond is 2.0 ponds ($800–4,000, 16 h).

**A drawn kind's catalogue twin** (`yard_kinds.twin_of`) is what the score, the Water page, the
hours and the buy list count: a pond, a rain garden, a rock garden, a native lawn, a swale, and a
bed **only when it is raised**. A fence, a wall and a building have none: a drawn one is most often
there already, as a drawn house always was. So a converted Tool Shed and Fence / Wall leave the buy
list and the care hours (3 and 4 h a year); nothing in the score moves, because neither was a
habitat structure.

## The scripting API

`Project.place_structure` keeps its signature **and still stores a point**: the generator builds
its plan through it, places its pond there and seats the water plants by it, and the app's plan
step reads those points (`controllers/generation._render`, which converts them as they reach the
map). Converting inside `place_structure` lost the generated pond on the way to the map, so the
plan changed here: **the API converts a copy when it saves** (`Project.save`), and a file it
writes is in the palette's form. Two new methods, `draw_area(kind, ring, **options)` and
`draw_line(kind, points, **options)`, are a deliberate change to the contract's snapshot. The MCP
tools are unchanged.

## What changed, measured

`scripts/render_structures.py --readers` (each case converted as the app opens it; `--as-stored`
reads it as V3.71 did) on a 12 × 12 m yard:

| On the map | V3.71 | V3.72 |
|---|---|---|
| A Garden Bed given 0.45 m | the whole yard to plant (144 m²), a building in 3D, a shade caster | a raised bed: 3.6 m² to plant, soil in a frame, no shade |
| A Tool Shed | a model, no shade | a building, 2.4 m, casts shade |
| A placed pond | the pond model | water at its outline (as a drawn pond since V3.45) |
| A raised bed | the model | soil lifted 45 cm in a frame along its outline |
| A rain garden, rock garden, native lawn | their models | a surface each (`rain_garden`, `rock`, `young_meadow`) |
| A swale | the model | a strip of the rain garden's look, at its width, kept out of by the planner |
| Every pair (placed and drawn) | agreed in the score, the Overview, Plan My Yard and the Water page (V3.71) | still agrees; the 3D now agrees too |

**Where it departs from the plan:**

- `place_structure` stores a point and the API converts on save (above).
- A fence, wall or building is not costed (above).
- **R also turns an object**, and **Y** opens Placement › Yard (S opened Structures). The R key
  turns the community being placed first, as V3.59 made it, and otherwise the object under the
  pointer.
- **Plants on a raised bed stand at ground level** inside its frame, as they stood inside the
  raised-bed model. V3.73 stands them on its soil.
- The objects keep a right-click menu (*Turn 45°*, *Remove*) where a right-click removed at once.
