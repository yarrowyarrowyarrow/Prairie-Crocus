# V3.74 · Hedges you can plant (F280, increment 4)

The owner: "go ahead with V3.74". The fourth of [the V3.71 plan](V3.71-one-palette.md)'s five
increments: **a hedge is plants.** Until now a hedge, a living fence and a windbreak were a green
line with a species typed into a box: nothing bought, scored, fed or shaded by it, and the 3D drew a
plain green mass the height of an estimate. This increment plants them with the region's shrubs and
trees, and makes a wall and a board fence cast shade (the owner's Q3).

## What a person sees

- **Placement › Yard › Hedge** asks for a shrub from a list of the region's shrubs; a **living
  fence** for two or three; a **windbreak** for a tree and a tall shrub. No width, no "how far
  apart", no free-text box: the shrub decides those.
- **Draw on the map**, double-click to finish: the line and its shrubs go down together, as **one
  undo step**. The shrubs are plants like any other, so the plant list, the buy list, the Overview,
  Through the year, the habitat score, the shade map, the wind shadow and the 3D's shrubs (V3.49)
  all see them, and a shrub's page says why it is there.
- **Right-click the line**: *Plant this hedge…* (choose the shrubs; planting again replaces the
  line's shrubs) and *Remove the hedge and its N shrubs*. Removing the line takes its shrubs; removing
  a shrub on its own leaves the line.
- **An old hedge is never planted on its own** (the owner's Q5, recommended): the Overview's *How
  full it will be* names it and the region's shrub its words match, with *Plant this hedge*, which
  opens the same choice with that shrub chosen.
- **A wall and a board fence cast shade** on the shade map, the planting zones, Plan My Yard and the
  timeline. A rail or chain-link fence does not.

## The rules (`src/hedge_planting.py`, Qt-free; P1)

Every number is a **stated estimate** in the module, read from the catalogue where it can be:

- **Which plants.** The region's list (`native_here.catalogue_scope`, as every plant list), never a
  plant that needs standing water. A hedge or a living fence: shrubs that stand (not a mat,
  `branching: prostrate`) and at least knee high (`HEDGE_MIN_M`, 0.75 m). A windbreak: a back row of
  trees and shrubs at least 4 m (`TALL_MIN_M`), a front row of shrubs at least 2 m (`FRONT_MIN_M`).
- **Which is chosen first.** Sold locally (`local_stock.easy_to_find`) and then the plant list's
  own order (`plant_priority.rank`); a windbreak's back row puts an evergreen first, because a
  windbreak is mostly for winter. Every choice is the person's to change.
- **How far apart.** The shrub's own spacing (`spacing_meters`). The catalogue's crown is 1.5 times
  it, so neighbours' crowns overlap by half a spacing once grown: **the hedge closes as it grows**,
  rather than being planted shoulder to shoulder on day one, which is the plan's "its spacing is the
  shrub's own". A line of length *L* takes *n* = round(*L* / spacing) plants (at least one), evenly,
  half a step in from each end, so a hedge drawn to a corner does not hang past it.
- **A living fence** repeats its two or three shrubs in order (A B C A B C) at their mean spacing.
- **A windbreak** is two rows either side of the line, apart by half the back row's spacing and at
  least 1.5 m (`ROW_GAP_SHARE`, `ROW_GAP_MIN_M`), each row at its own spacing, so they stagger. The
  back row stands on the side facing more north, so it does not stand between the shrubs and the
  midday sun.
- **Its words.** An old hedge's typed species is read against the candidates by
  `stock_lists.match`, the reader that already takes a nursery's names, renames and old names.

Each plant goes in through `project_store` with `placement_group_id` the line's `hedge_id`,
`pattern_kind` `hedge` and `why_here` (`why_here.HEDGE`). The line keeps the chosen `plant_ids`,
which is what *Plant this hedge…* opens with next time. **Whether a line is planted is read from
its plants**, never stored: delete every shrub by hand and the line is an unplanted hedge again.

## Shade (Q3)

`yard_kinds.shade_height` is the one answer: a wall (`WALL_HEIGHT_M`, 1.2 m) and a board fence
(`FENCE_HEIGHT_M["board"]`, 1.8 m) cast; a rail and a chain-link fence let the light through. The
heights moved from `scene_kinds` and `scene_site` into `yard_kinds`, so the 3D and the shade map
read one number. `shade.casters_from_project` casts each segment as a strip `SHADE_STRIP_M` (0.2 m)
wide extruded to that height, the same as a building's footprint, so the shade map, the planting
zones, `SuccessionEngine`'s fixed casters and Plan My Yard's last step (`plan_shade`) all see it
from one change. A fence is not in Sun & Shade's tick list (`sun_shade.caster_rows`): left out, it
has no switch.

## In 3D

A planted line draws nothing (`scene_site.lines` skips it); its plants are the shrubs and trees
V3.49 and V3.50 built. An old, unplanted hedge is still the plain green mass.

## Left out, on purpose

- **Moving a hedge** moves its line only (F279 will move the line and its plants together).
- **Clipped hedges.** The shrubs are drawn at their own grown shape; nothing records a hedge kept
  to a height, and drawing one clipped would state what the person has not said.
- **The windbreak's side from the wind.** The wind rose is fetched on the pin and cached, but a
  side chosen by it would move with every fetch; north is fixed and states its reason.
- **The scripting API's `draw_line`** keeps its V3.72 contract: it draws a line. Planting it is
  `hedge_planting.plant`, which the API's caller can call on its project.

## What was built, measured

- **The lists.** On the Edmonton list: 30 shrubs for a hedge or a living fence, 18 for a
  windbreak's back row (10 trees and 8 shrubs at least 4 m), 17 for its front row. First choices
  by the rule: Chokecherry for a hedge; Chokecherry and Pussy Willow for a living fence; Jack Pine
  and Chokecherry for a windbreak.
- **Along a 20 m line**: 7 Chokecherry (spacing 3 m), 8 Saskatoon Berry (2.5 m), 10 Red Osier
  Dogwood (2 m), 20 Common Snowberry (1 m), each half a step in from the ends.
- **An old hedge's words**: "saskatoon" and "Amelanchier alnifolia" read as Saskatoon Berry,
  "dogwood" as Red Osier Dogwood, "spruce" on a windbreak as White Spruce in its back row and
  "dogwood" there as its front row; "lilac" matches nothing (not native here), and the offer opens
  on the first choices.
- **Shade.** On a 1 m grid, the three rows of ground north of a 1.8 m board fence are shaded for
  100%, 67% and 22% of the sampled moments; nothing south of it; a rail fence casts nothing. A
  full-sun penstemon a metre north of a board fence is shaded 78% of them, and the timeline's note
  says "the fence", not "a building" (`what` rides on the caster through `SuccessionEngine`).
- **In 3D** (`scripts/render_yard.py --design` on a 20 x 34 m lot with a house, a saskatoon hedge,
  a dogwood, snowberry and cinquefoil living fence, a spruce and chokecherry windbreak, a board
  fence and a wall): the three lines are their shrubs and trees, the windbreak's two rows
  staggered, and no green line mass is drawn for any of them.
- `tests/test_hedge_planting.py` holds the rules, the store, the offer, the shade and the window's
  side with a fake window; `tests/test_yard_map.py` reads the line's menu and dots off the real map
  in headless Chromium; `tests/test_yard_palette.py` the plant lists.

**Where it departs from the plan:** the line's right-click had removed it at once; it is now a
menu, for every line, so a fence's menu holds only Remove (as an object's did from V3.72). The
status line after drawing says "Line drawn: 12.0 m, 5 plants" rather than "Hedgerow placed".

## What could not be checked here

- **The real window.** This container has no QtWebEngine, so the drawn line, its plants appearing
  on the map and *Plant this hedge…* were run through the handlers with a fake window and the real
  map page in headless Chromium, not clicked in the app. CI's smoke test builds the window.
- **The first choices.** The plant list's own order puts most wildlife first, which makes a 5 m
  Chokecherry the first hedge. Whether a homeowner pictures that as a hedge is for the owner.
