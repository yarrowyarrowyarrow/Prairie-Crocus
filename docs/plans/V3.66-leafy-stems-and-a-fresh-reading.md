# V3.66 · Leafy stems, and all 250 read again (F268)

**The owner:** "What work is left that can bring the average score up even more?", then "Go
ahead" with the answer's recommendation: the leafy stems first, the biggest real gain, and a
fresh reading of all 250 beside it, so the average is measured against what the viewer draws now.

## Why these two

After V3.65 the average over the 250 natives was 5.15. Weighted by how far each plant falls short
of 7 (the score at which a model does its job), the largest fault was `sparse` (54 plants) with
`stem_leaves` (18) beside it: 147 points, nearly all on the 105 wildflowers. And 154 of the 250
readings were V3.62's, made before the shrub, leaf-colour and flower fixes of V3.63 to V3.65; 58
of them still carried a `leaf_colour` fault for plants whose colour had since been read. The
average was understating the models by an unknown amount.

## 1. Leafy stems

**What was measured.** A swamp aster is drawn as 6 stems of 25 leaves, 11 cm by 2.4 cm: about
0.024 m² of leaf over an outline of about 1.6 m², under 2%. Its photographs show a leafy mass.
The budget was not the limit (1,776 of 1,800 triangles, nothing cut): the counted leaves are all
the leaf there is. A stem also carries the leaves of its side shoots and axils, which nobody
counted, and from a yard away the plant is the mass they make.

**What was drawn.** A new chunk, `html/scene3d/03g-stemleaves.js`. A leafy wildflower's stems (the
`stems` and `colony` forms of `03c-leafy.js`) carry cut-out cards of its own leafy shoots
(`_wdSpray`: its leaf outline, in its arrangement), as a shrub's leaves have been since V3.49 and a
mat's since V3.65: set up each stem between its lowest leaf and its flowers, standing out and up
from the stem (the lower ones spreading wider), steeper where they would pass the plant's
outline, every tip under the stem's top. As many as cover the outline, less what the counted
leaves already cover. Stated estimates, in the chunk's header:

- `HERB_COVER` 0.4: the share of its outline a leafy wildflower fills in leaf (a shrub's is 0.75);
- `HERB_CARD_BUDGET` 500 to 2,200 triangles from 0.3 m to 1.5 m tall at Balanced, 1.6 times that at
  Lifelike;
- `HERB_GAIN_MAX` 1.6: how much larger than life a card may be drawn to cover it;
- `_HERB_LEAF_FILL`: how much of its length by width a divided leaf fills (a pinnate leaf 0.35):
  counted as solid, a milk-vetch's leaves seemed to cover it already and it got none.

A stem of one or two leaves gets none (`HERB_CARD_MIN_NODES` 3): a sarsaparilla's single
umbrella, a lily's pair, are what the readings say they are. Rosettes, tufts and mats are
unchanged. Stylised draws none of this.

**Found on the way.** The first build let a card near the top of a stem reach above the plant.
The body is scaled to its highest point, so every stemmed wildflower shrank by up to 17%; the
leafy render test caught it (its height and flower-tip checks). Every card now ends under the
stem's top.
