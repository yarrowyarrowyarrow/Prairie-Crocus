# V3.56 · One plant page (F271)

*The owner, 18 November 2026: "Next I'd like a review of the plant panel. This may be one of the
most useful aspects of the app for people to learn about and select native plants. Currently it is
overwhelming with data and simply too much. Again it could be reduced by 50% or more especially any
redundant information. Ensure the most useful info is presented first. Ask me questions if needed.
I know i said remove data but also consider what might possibly be useful to add."*

The screenshots were of the **plant page** (`src/species_page.py`, the frame beside the plant list),
with Advanced tools on, which showed the long page from the start. With them off the page was
already about a hundred words and an *Everything about this plant ▸* button (PR-4, V3.26).

What the long page said more than once, or should not have said:

- **What it feeds, three times**: *Why it matters* (Keystone · Hosts 4 caterpillars · Bird food),
  *Roles* (the Role filter's tags: Keystone Species · Larval Host · Bird Food · Wildlife Habitat ·
  Early Successional) and *Wildlife* (Feeds or shelters 6 documented species, then the names).
- **Safety, twice**: "Not checked for poison" and "Toxicity not assessed — absence of a warning is
  not a guarantee of safety".
- **The months, twice**: "blooms Apr–May" written over the bar that draws April and May.
- **Native, three layers**: *Native to* ("as VASCAN records it"), *Around Edmonton*, *Found in*
  (four ecoregions with counts and confidence, and a "Range data: GBIF …" line).
- **Sources, a third of the page**: three full citations, then *Where these numbers came from*
  with eight iNaturalist photograph numbers, printed twice (the leaf line repeated the flower line)
  and an internal version tag, "(read V3.50)".
- **"AB retail as of 2026"** under *Where to get it*: a phrase the glossary (`vocabulary.NEVER`,
  "AB retail estimate") and the printout (`planting_plan.price_words`) already keep out.
- **A false reason**: "Flowers in April or May, when early bees need it" on Water Birch. The early-
  flowers point skipped grasses, sedges, rushes and six wetland genera, and nothing else wind-
  pollinated. Birches, poplars, aspen, alders, hazel, spruce, pine, tamarack, fir, juniper, oak,
  elm, sage and winterfat all took the point and the sentence: 28 catalogue rows.
- **"Berries or seed for 2 kinds of bird"** on a birch, which has no berries; the records say which.

## What the owner decided

Asked four questions:

1. **One page, or two?** "I want the design details on this short page like time of year, etc. This
   may be more important than the other 2. But one page is best." So: one page in both modes, the
   design details (sun, soil, size, hardiness, colours, form, the season bar, the calendar's jobs)
   **on** it; the animal list and where it grows wild, the other two, in folds.
2. **The wind-pollinated trees**: fix it, knowing some drop a band.
3. **Name two or three animals** in *Why this plant*: yes, by the Overview's rule.
4. **Additions** (a *Try instead* for a plant no local list carries, autumn colour, a link to the
   website's range map, *Grows wild with*): "None of these speak to me." None added.

## What the page is now

One page (`plant_page_sections.simple`, drawn by `SpeciesPage._render`), Advanced tools adding only
Add to mix and the ID line under the photographs (W3 kept):

1. The band.
2. **Why this plant**: the ranking's reasons, with up to three animals named, two to a reason at
   most (`plant_page_sections.named_reasons`): *"Caterpillar food for 4 kinds of moth and
   butterfly, among them Mourning Cloak and Canadian Tiger Swallowtail."* Names come from
   `through_the_year.names_for`, the Overview's rule: one species with an English name, recorded
   near the place inside a pack, familiar first, then the most recorded near it. A reason never ends
   "and 1 more": the second name is shown instead.
3. **Good to know**: the cautions, poison said once, edible parts.
4. **Growing it**: sun and soil, hardy to zone N, size, spacing, the calendar's jobs ("Prune in
   March", "Harvest in July and August" for an edible plant). The jobs are `month_tasks.page_line`,
   the status bar's table, so the two cannot disagree.
5. **Looks like**: flower colour (fruit colour where it is a word, not a hex), the leaves and form,
   "(a guess from its genus)" only where the value is an estimate, then the season bar. The
   months are the bar's alone.
6. **Buying it**, then **Native around Edmonton.**
7. **Animals recorded on it (N) ▸**: by what they take, eight names a group then a link, the
   specialists flagged, the sources as short citations on one line ("Acorn & Sheldon 2006; Pohl et
   al. 2018").
8. **Where it grows wild ▸**: native to (the provinces), around Edmonton with its collections, the
   four regions most recorded with their counts, "and N more regions", the retrieval.

Both folds start closed and **stay as the reader left them from one plant to the next**, so two
plants can be compared with the same fold open.

Gone from the page: *Why it matters*, *Roles*, *Conditions*, *Size* as a list, the months written
out, *Grows with* (companion lore, never on the short page), *Where to get it*'s estimate note, the
second safety line, *Where these numbers came from*. The ranking, the website and the 3D cards
still read every field; only this page stops repeating them.

## The wind-pollinated trees

`plant_priority.WIND_GENERA` gains *Betula, Alnus, Populus, Corylus, Quercus, Ulmus, Fraxinus,
Picea, Pinus, Larix, Abies, Pseudotsuga, Juniperus, Artemisia, Krascheninnikovia*, and
`WIND_SPECIES` *Acer negundo*. **Willows are left in**: bees work their catkins, and the test holds
that a willow still takes the point. `flower_form = 'none'` was considered as the test and rejected:
it also marks Spring Beauty, Fairy Bells, Sweet Cicely and the buffaloberries, which bees visit.

28 rows lose a point. Four Edmonton natives change band, all **Good pick → Little recorded**: White
Spruce, Thinleaf Alder, Prairie Sage, Plains Wormwood. Water Birch, Paper Birch, Trembling Aspen and
Balsam Poplar stay Top picks (their caterpillar floor holds them). The generator reads the same
ranking, so a plan may choose a different plant where these were tied.

## What was left alone

- **`forage_calendar.WIND_POLLINATED_TYPES`** is the same three types, and may count a birch as
  bee forage in its month. Not checked here; it is the Habitat page's, not the plant page's.
- **"as VASCAN records it"** is `native_here.province_words`, shared with the website, and now
  sits inside a closed fold. Not reworded.
- **The photograph credit** is iNaturalist's attribution string as stored.
- **A click on a label draws the focus ring round it** (the yellow box on the scientific name and
  *Safety* in the owner's screenshots): the labels are selectable, which is the V3.00 decision.

## Ceilings

`plant_priority.py` 380 → 400 (386 now: the wind genera, the bird food word, the reasons' animals).
`plant_page_sections.py` stays under 250 by moving the calendar line into `month_tasks` and the
naming into `through_the_year`, where the rules they share already live.
